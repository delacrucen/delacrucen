## 📌 What I do
- Ship banking and payments apps to production: a mobile wallet published on the App Store and Google Play (OTA updates, App Review included) and a banking app that runs on POS terminals.
- Write native modules in Swift, Kotlin and C++ for camera, video and POS hardware when React Native is not enough.
- Usually own the frontend end to end: the architecture, the CI/CD and release pipeline and the patterns the rest of the team builds on.
- Bridge design and code: Figma to production, design systems and design feedback for UI/UX teams.
- Build my own products: a livestock marketplace app with live auctions, a music streaming PWA with synced lyrics, a multi-tenant CRM for gyms and more.
- Take on freelance projects: online raffle platforms for brand campaigns with local payment gateways, mobile apps, corporate sites and landing pages for companies and software studios.
